How to interpret this dashboard

Sales values are the sum of non-missing Amount values. AOV is the mean of per-order recorded Amount totals. Order counts use distinct Order IDs. Segment and fulfilment shares use recorded Amount.

The source does not include reliable customer IDs, product/SKU IDs, costs, fees, margins, or delivery timestamps. Retention, customer lifetime value, product profitability, profit margin, and delivery duration are therefore not calculated.
